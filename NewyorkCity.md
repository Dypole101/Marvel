**NEWYORKCITY - HOW TO PLAY**

Touch: left side drag = move · right side drag = look · **USE** = fire the selected power (hold for beams) · **NEXT** = switch power · PORTAL / HAMMER = permanent buttons for those heroes · JUMP · FLY · SPRINT (toggle) · SHIELD (hold)

PC: W A S D move · mouse drag look · Space jump · C sprint · G fly · Q shield (hold) · F use power (hold) · X next power · 1-7 pick a power · H this panel\
Direct keys: E portal · M mirror · T time stone · R rewind (hold)

Choose a hero: walk into the big tower and stand on a glowing circle - Sorcerer, Inventor or Thunder God. The two armor circles pick Striker or Nano armor.

Rewind: by default REWIND undoes everything around you (robots, portals, bolts, moved buildings) but NOT you. Inside the Mirror Dimension a REWIND ME button appears at the top so you can choose to rewind yourself too.\
Sorcerer: PORTAL (always on its own button), then tap the ground, a roof or a wall - the exit opens there. SAVE stores your spot, POSITIONS opens a portal to a saved spot. MIRROR makes a mirror wall; then SELECT + arrows move buildings, or tap the floor to grab a flat area of it (floor areas move flat only). Press MIRROR again to restore everything. TIME slows the world, REWIND (hold) undoes everything. Cloak flight with FLY.

Inventor: CALL SUIT summons the armor (Nano grows from the chest reactor, Striker flies in). MASK raises/lowers the helmet (HUD shows only with the helmet down). EJECT removes the armor. REPULSOR, ROCKETS (homing), LASER (hold). SHIELD makes an energy shield. FLY needs the suit.

Thunder God: the HAMMER button throws the hammer, then press it again to call it back (V on PC). BOLT (chain lightning), STORM (sky strikes at the pointer), STRIKE to smash. FLY and SHIELD need the hammer in hand.

You can stand on roofs. Robots: brawlers hit, gunners shoot, flyers hover and fire. Enemies can't enter the tower.
